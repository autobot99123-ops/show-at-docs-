Your Identity

Techy

Techy is your technical identity: skills, connected platforms, projects, and streaks — visualized as an interactive node canvas.

What Techy contains

  • Skills and tools — what you list, plus which of your projects actually use each skill.
  • Connected platforms — GitHub, LeetCode, Kaggle, HackerRank, LinkedIn. See Connected Accounts.
  • Projects — your published work with lifecycle status.
  • Streaks — your ShowAt build streak plus per-platform activity streaks and year-grid activity nodes.
  • Community — your attach counts.

The Techy canvas

The canvas draws every item as its own node, grouped by family — skills, projects, streaks, social, community — with clear gaps between families so a full canvas never mixes them.

  • Drag nodes to arrange them; edges follow.
  • Drag the background to pan; pinch, scroll, or use the buttons to zoom.
  • Tap a hub to collapse or expand its family; tap an item to open it.
  • Fullscreen, minimap, and list view are built in via the canvas toolbar.

Streaks and activity grids

Provider streak nodes show live day counts with a GitHub-style year grid where day data exists: GitHub shows a trailing ~90-day window (the limit of its public API), LeetCode shows a full year. Squares you see are real synced days; blank squares are days with no recorded activity — never invented.
