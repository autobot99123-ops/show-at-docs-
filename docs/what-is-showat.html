Getting Started

What is ShowAt?

ShowAt is a student-focused platform where your technical identity, projects, and community activity live together.

In simple language

As a student, your work is scattered: code on GitHub, problem-solving on LeetCode, projects in folders, skills in your head. ShowAt gives you one student-focused layer to present all of it — a profile for who you are, Techy for what you can do, projects for what you built, and a community to share it with.

ShowAt and external platforms

ShowAt does not replace other platforms. The relationship is:

  • GitHub remains the source of code. ShowAt links your repositories and reads your public activity.
  • LeetCode remains the source of problem-solving activity. ShowAt reads your public submission calendar.
  • Other platforms remain their own sources of truth. ShowAt links them and presents them together.

ShowAt is the presentation layer; the platforms stay the source of the underlying work. See Connected Accounts for exactly what syncs from where.

The parts of ShowAt

  • Profile — basic identity, posts, projects, Techy, and community connections.
  • Techy — skills, connected platforms, and a visual canvas of your technical world.
  • Projects — things you built, with lifecycle status, milestones, and progress.
  • Community — discovering students, posts, tools, resources, attaching, and messaging.
